Our kitchen

One recipe, cooked the same way every time

Crispy & Crunch started opposite SBI Bank in Mahua with one goal: fast food that tastes as good as the big chains, at a price everyone here can afford.

Now we're writing every recipe down to the gram, the minute and the oil temperature, so the chicken you love here tastes exactly the same in every new outlet we open.

Franchise

Bring Crispy & Crunch to your town

We're opening franchise outlets in phases, grouped in nearby towns so every partner gets proper support. Each partner receives the complete franchise pack before signing: agreement, operations manual, recipes, approved vendors and a training plan.

10outlets planned in the first phase
2pilot outlets open first, so the system is proven
1–2 wkshands-on training in our Mahua kitchen

What you get

Training for you and your staff

Kitchen, service, billing and daily reporting, taught in our own shop.

Operations manual

Step-by-step SOPs for opening, frying, hygiene, stock, cash and complaints.

Tested recipes

Every item written with exact grams, temperatures and timings.

Supply you can rely on

Our signature spice mix and sauces, plus an approved vendor list with backups.

Outlet design pack

Layout, equipment list, signage and packaging, ready to fit out.

Launch and ongoing support

Our team on site in launch week, brand marketing, and regular visits after.

How it works

  1. EnquireFill in the form below or message us.
  2. Intro callWe talk about your plans, budget and location.
  3. Site checkWe review your site against our checklist.
  4. SignAgreement and full franchise pack, reviewed together.
  5. Train and openTraining in Mahua, then we open with you.

Who we're looking for

  • Owners who will be involved in running the outlet
  • Funds for setup and working capital
  • A busy location, owned or rented, or the will to find one
  • Respect for the recipe: same standards, every day

Investment, fees and the support we provide are set out in our franchise information pack, which we share after the intro call.
